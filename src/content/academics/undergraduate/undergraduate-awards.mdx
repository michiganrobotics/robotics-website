During the Fall semester, undergraduate students can apply for the College of Engineering's Leaders and Honors Awards.

For descriptions of each award, please see the **[Award and Prize Description page](https://studentawards.engin.umich.edu/student-awards/undergraduate-student-awards/).**
Students may [self-nominate](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSemWpPg26urc4-zaMcVMcuwsUdgLNLR39DOwHQWjYf4_btKAQ/viewform?usp=sf_link) for multiple awards each year.

## College of Engineering Leaders and Honors Awards

The application for 2026-2027 awards is expected to become available in October.

<div class="surface-accent rounded-lg p-6 mb-6">

Declared Robotics students can also use the nomination intake form below to communicate your achievements to the Robotics Undergraduate Committee to help with matching students and the available awards. We recommend that all Robotics students fill this out, whether you know which award you align best with or not.

This can be updated at any time as you grow throughout your undergraduate career – we recommend updating your information at least once per academic year!
