The Robotics Department provides limited funding to support Robotics students through academic, professional, community engagement, and outreach activities. Examples include competitions, conferences, student-led community events, outreach programs, and project or educational materials.

Annual funding levels vary, and awards depend on availability. Requests are evaluated by committee and may be submitted only during the Fall and Winter terms (from the first day of Fall classes through the last day of Winter classes).

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6 my-8">

### Emergency funding requests

Students facing unexpected financial hardship due to circumstances such as lost wages or medical or family emergencies can explore emergency funding through:

- [College of Engineering](https://scholarships.engin.umich.edu/emergency-fund/)
- [Dean of Students](https://deanofstudents.umich.edu/article/student-emergency-funds)
- [CEW+](https://cew.umich.edu/funding/emergency-funding/)
- [The Office of the Provost's list of additional resources](https://provost.umich.edu/resources-policies/student-resources/emergency-funds/)

</div>

<h2 class="text-3xl font-bold font-roboto-condensed mt-0">Funding categories and deadlines</h2>

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-8">

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6">

### Student organizations: Fall deadline

Registered student organizations have one annual deadline: **the first Monday of the second month of the Fall term**.

Late requests are considered only for unforeseeable, time-sensitive opportunities and only if funds remain, at the committee's discretion.

</div>

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6">

### Individuals and teams: Rolling requests

After annual student organization awards are made, remaining funds may be available for rolling requests on a case-by-case basis, including:

- Individuals attending competitions or conferences
- Student teams or project groups not applying as registered student organizations
- Time-sensitive needs, such as registration, required materials, or allowable travel

Rolling requests depend on remaining funds.

</div>

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6 lg:col-span-2">

### Community engagement and outreach

Funding is available to support activities that strengthen the Robotics community or engage external communities. Eligible activities include:

- Outreach events led by Robotics students
- Community-building events for groups within Robotics
- Development of materials or resources that support community engagement and outreach

Two funding cycles are available:

- **Requests up to $1,500:** Due by the first Monday of the second month of the applicable Fall or Winter term
- **Requests up to $500:** Accepted on a rolling basis during the Fall and Winter terms, subject to available funds

</div>

### Who is eligible

**Student organizations**

Organizations must:

- Be registered U-M Ann Arbor student organizations in good standing
- Include at least 5% Robotics students

**Individuals and student teams**

Applicants must:

- Be currently enrolled, U-M Ann Arbor affiliated students
- Be primarily composed of or serve Robotics degree-seeking students
- Demonstrate clear Robotics educational or professional-development value with defined outcomes

**Community engagement and outreach applicants**

Applicants must be Robotics students, student teams, or student organizations proposing an activity or resource that:

- Supports outreach led by Robotics students
- Builds community among groups within Robotics
- Develops materials or resources for community engagement and outreach

</div>

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6">

### What we fund

Funding may support:

- Competition registration and allowable travel
- Conferences aligned with Robotics education
- Project materials and supplies tied to defined deliverables
- Outreach and educational initiatives engaging students or the broader community
- Community-building events for groups within Robotics
- Materials and resources that support community engagement or outreach

</div>

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6">

### What we do not fund

These funds will not be used for:

- Summer internship stipends or internship travel
- Living costs (housing, rent, meals, general cost of living)
- Expenses that cannot be purchased or reimbursed through approved university processes
- Wages, health insurance, gifts, or personal honoraria unless explicitly approved and compliant with university policy

</div>

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6 lg:col-span-2">

### Request checklist

**All requests** include:

- **Description and alignment:** What you propose, why it matters, relevant dates, and how Robotics students will benefit
- **Itemized budget:** Line items, estimates, and dates using the [budget template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1_wyCNdXJvpOVdhdIt21nXIg3jnB63oRceMnnL7b4EeA/copy)
- **Requested amount:** Amount requested from Robotics and costs covered by other sources
- **Other funding sources:** Applied, confirmed, or pending
- **Participants:** Names, U-M affiliations, roles, or estimated number of people affected
- **Outcome plan:** Examples include a demonstration, write-up, presentation, resource, or outreach metrics
- **Timing constraints:** Relevant deadlines or urgency, especially for rolling requests

**Student organizations** also include:

- A full-year budget covering Fall and Winter, and Spring or Summer only when requesting support for eligible activities during those terms
- A cost-sharing plan, such as sponsors, fundraising, dues, or College of Engineering support
- Confirmation of registration and good standing
- Confirmation that at least 5% of members are Robotics students
- A summary of efforts to obtain other appropriate funding and the outcomes, if known

**Community engagement and outreach requests** also include:

- The intended internal or external community
- Expected participation or reach
- A description of how Robotics students will lead or contribute
- The anticipated community or outreach benefit
- A plan for sharing, maintaining, or reusing materials and resources, when applicable

</div>

<div class="surface rounded-lg p-6 lg:col-span-2">

### Review, decisions, and award requirements

Requests are reviewed by:

- The Robotics Undergraduate Committee
- The Robotics Graduate Committee
- The appropriate committee responsible for community engagement and outreach requests

**Decision criteria** include impact and access, alignment with the funding purpose, budget clarity, feasibility, timing, cost sharing, and prior awards in the same academic year.

**Notification timing**

- Student organization annual cycle: within three weeks after the deadline
- Individual and team rolling requests: reviewed as received; timing depends on urgency and committee availability
- Community engagement and outreach requests up to $1,500: timing depends on the committee's review schedule following each deadline
- Community engagement and outreach requests up to $500: reviewed on a rolling basis, subject to committee availability

**If you receive funding**, recipients must:

- Use funds only as approved
- Follow university purchasing and reimbursement procedures
- Obtain prior approval for material changes in scope or purpose
- Submit a year-end expense report to robotics-sso@umich.edu, attention: Student Services Manager
- If appropriate and permission is granted, document the project or event with photos or video and share them with the Robotics Communications Manager

</div>
